# Superstore Sales Interactive Dashboard – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is part of my **Data Analyst Internship at SWYNEX Technologies**.

In the previous task, the Superstore Sales dataset was cleaned and prepared for analysis. In this task, I used that **cleaned dataset in Microsoft Power BI** to create an interactive sales dashboard.

The main goal was to transform the cleaned sales data into an easy-to-understand interactive dashboard that provides a complete overview of sales performance across different time periods, categories, regions, customers, products, and locations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this Power BI project were to:

- Load the previously cleaned Superstore Sales dataset into Power BI.
- Create important sales KPIs.
- Analyze sales performance using different business dimensions.
- Create interactive charts and visualizations.
- Add Year and Month filters.
- Build a clean and user-friendly interactive dashboard.
- Present important sales information in an easy-to-understand format.

---

# 🔄 Power BI Dashboard Workflow

## Step 1 – Load the Cleaned Dataset

I opened **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** and loaded the cleaned Superstore Sales dataset.

### Process:

1. Opened Power BI Desktop.
2. Selected **Get Data**.
3. Selected the cleaned Superstore dataset.
4. Loaded the dataset into Power BI.
5. Reviewed the available fields and data types.
6. Used the required fields to create the dashboard visuals.

The cleaned dataset served as the main data source for the complete dashboard.

---

# 📌 Step 2 – Create KPI Cards

I created KPI cards at the top of the dashboard to provide a quick summary of the overall sales performance.

### Total Sales

**₹22,61,536.78**

This represents the total sales generated across the complete dataset.

### Total Orders

**4,922**

This represents the distinct number of orders.

### Total Customers

**793**

This represents the distinct number of customers.

These KPI cards allow users to understand the overall business performance immediately.

---

# 📈 Step 3 – Sales by Year

I created a **Sales by Year** line chart using `Order_Date` and `Sales`.

This visualization shows how sales changed from year to year and makes it easier to identify overall yearly sales trends.

---

# 📊 Step 4 – Sales by Category

I created a **Sales by Category** chart to compare sales across the three major product categories:

- Technology
- Furniture
- Office Supplies

This helps identify which product category contributes the most to total sales.

---

# 🚚 Step 5 – Sales by Ship Mode

I created a **Sales by Ship Mode** chart to analyze sales across different shipping methods:

- Standard Class
- Second Class
- First Class
- Same Day

This provides a clear view of sales performance across different shipping modes.

---

# 📅 Step 6 – Monthly Sales Trend

I created a **Monthly Sales Trend** line chart using the `Order_Date` field.

The date hierarchy was used to display monthly sales from:

**January → December**

This visualization helps users understand how sales change throughout the year and identify months with higher or lower sales.

---

# 🌎 Step 7 – Sales by Region

I created a **Sales by Region** chart to compare sales performance across:

- West
- East
- Central
- South

This allows users to understand which geographical region contributes more to overall sales.

---

# 👥 Step 8 – Sales by Segment

I created a **Sales by Segment** chart to analyze sales across different customer segments:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

This helps compare the contribution of different customer segments to total sales.

---

# 📍 Step 9 – Sales by State

I created a **Sales by State** visualization to analyze sales performance across different states.

This allows users to identify states with relatively higher or lower sales.

---

# 🏷️ Step 10 – Sales by Sub-Category

I created a **Sales by Sub-Category** bar chart to compare sales across different product sub-categories.

This provides a more detailed view of product-level performance beyond the main categories.

---

# 🏙️ Step 11 – Sales by City

I created a **Sales by City** visualization to analyze sales across different cities.

This helps identify cities contributing significantly to overall sales.

---

# 📦 Step 12 – Sales by Product Name

I created a **Sales by Product Name** visualization to analyze sales at the individual product level.

This helps identify products generating higher sales within the dataset.

---

# 🎛️ Step 13 – Add Interactive Slicers

To make the dashboard interactive, I added two slicers:

### Year Slicer

Allows users to select a specific year and analyze the corresponding sales performance.

### Month Slicer

Allows users to filter the dashboard according to specific months.

These slicers make it possible to interactively explore the dashboard instead of viewing only static results.
